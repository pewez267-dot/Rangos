# SwitchTune — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 June 2026_

SwitchTune is designed to do one thing — convert a music link from one streaming
service to another — while collecting **as little data as possible**.

## Summary

- **No account, no login.** SwitchTune never asks you to sign up or sign in.
- **No personal data collected.** We do not collect your name, email, location,
  contacts, or any personally identifiable information.
- **No analytics or advertising SDKs.** There are no ads and no third-party
  tracking in the app.
- **No servers of our own.** SwitchTune has no backend. Your preferences stay on
  your device.

## What data is processed, and where

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|------|----------------|-----|
| Your preferred music service | On your device only (local storage) | To know which app to open |
| The music link you share or copy | Sent to the Odesli API to find matches | Core function of the app |
| Clipboard text (only when you open the app) | Read on-device, only to detect a music link | Convenience (Path B) |

The music link you share is sent to **Odesli (song.link)**, a third-party
service, solely to retrieve the equivalent links on other platforms. We send
only the link itself. Please review Odesli's privacy policy at
https://odesli.co/ for how they handle requests.

## Clipboard access

When you open SwitchTune, it may read your clipboard **once** to detect whether
you copied a music link. If the clipboard does not contain a recognised music
link, the content is ignored and never stored or transmitted.

## Children

SwitchTune is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Payments

SwitchTune is a **paid app** — the one-time price is charged by **Google Play**
at download. We never see or store your payment details, and there are no in-app
purchases or subscriptions.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at
https://switchtune.app/privacy with a new "Last updated" date.
