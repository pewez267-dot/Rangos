## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Got a song link from a friend, but it opens in the wrong app? SwitchTune fixes
that.

Share or paste a music link from one service and SwitchTune instantly finds the
same song on your preferred app and opens it — in one tap.

WHY SWITCHTUNE
- One-time purchase. No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts.
- Nothing to sign up for, nothing tracked about you.
- Works straight from the Android share menu, or from your clipboard.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your favourite music app once.
2. Share a song to SwitchTune (or copy a link and open the app).
3. Tap to open the song in your app. Done.

SUPPORTED SERVICES
Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, YouTube, Amazon Music, Deezer, and Tidal.

WHEN A MATCH ISN'T PERFECT
- If the song isn't available on your preferred app, SwitchTune offers to search
  for it there instead.
- If your preferred app isn't installed, it opens the song on the web.

PRIVACY FIRST
SwitchTune has no backend of its own. Your preferred-service choice stays on
your device. The only thing sent over the network is the link you want to
convert, which is matched using the Odesli (song.link) service.
